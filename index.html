Welcome to the CCMP Lab

The Computational Condensed Matter Physics (CCMP) Research Lab is dedicated to advancing the frontiers of materials science, nanotechnology, solid-state physics, and energy materials. With a strong focus on interdisciplinary research, the group is actively engaged in both experimental and theoretical investigations that address key scientific and technological challenges.

Led by Prof. Prafulla K. Jha, the group carries out first-principles (density functional theory, DFT) computational studies of a wide range of materials — from graphene and other carbon allotropes, boron nitride, metal and semiconductor nanoparticles and nanowires, complex oxides, nuclear materials, and transition-metal alloys and heterostructures — combined with low-frequency Raman spectroscopic studies and an ever-growing component of machine-learning-assisted materials design.

The group has published about 404 research papers in international journals, 102 conference proceedings, 7 edited books, 7 book chapters and 4 review articles, with over 10,900 citations and an h-index of 53.
