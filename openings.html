Ph.D. Positions

We welcome motivated students with a strong background in physics and an interest in computational condensed-matter physics and materials science. Research topics cover two-dimensional materials, topological quantum materials, energy and hydrogen-storage materials, nanophononics, and machine-learning-assisted materials design, using first-principles DFT codes and high-performance computing.

Interested candidates should contact Prof. Prafulla K. Jha with a detailed CV, a brief statement of research interest and copies of academic transcripts. Admission is through the university's Ph.D. entrance process.

Project Fellowships

The group frequently has project positions funded by DST, SERB, UGC, DAE-BRNS, DST-FIST and ANRF. Watch this page and the university announcements for current openings.

Collaborations

We actively collaborate with leading experimental and theoretical groups worldwide, including the Bialystok University of Technology (Poland), the Warsaw University of Technology (Poland), IIT Bombay (ANRF-PAIR Hub), and many others. We are open to new collaborative proposals in materials science and condensed-matter physics.
