Ferroelectric Rashba Topological Phase in Noncentrosymmetric CsSiBi

Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 035501 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

S. Patel, P. K. Jha, "Ferroelectric Rashba Topological Phase in Noncentrosymmetric CsSiBi," Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 035501 (2026).

DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/ae3511

In this work, we explore first-principles approaches to uncover the interplay of symmetry, spin–orbit coupling, topology, and ferroelectricity in noncentrosymmetric quantum materials. CsSiBi is coined as an intrinsic composite quantum compound with a topological insulating phase, isotropic Rashba spin-splitting and ferroelectric switching with large polarization and a low switching barrier. Our study further highlights the importance of full-potential codes for reliably describing the Rashba effect. The confluence of the aforementioned properties opens avenues for next-generation multifunctional materials for spintronics and nanoelectronics.
