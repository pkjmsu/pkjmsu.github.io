Lactoferrin as an Active Coordination Scaffold for Ruthenium (III)

Inorganic Chemistry Frontiers 13(11), 4867–4897 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

T. Dyrda-Terniuk, M. Ehlert, K. Roszak, G. Trykowski, K. Szpotkowski, Ł. Skowroński, O. Pryshchepa, R. van Eldik, K. Mizgalska, W. Guida, A. Karolak, R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, P. Pomastowski, "Lactoferrin as an active coordination scaffold for ruthenium (III)," Inorganic Chemistry Frontiers 13(11), 4867–4897 (2026).

DOI: 10.1039/d6qi00255b

This work investigates the molecular mechanism of Ru(III) coordination with bovine lactoferrin (bLF) and its structural and biological consequences. It combines molecular docking, molecular dynamics, DFT, spectroscopy, microscopy, SAXS, and ICP-OES to establish the binding mechanism. Ru(III) binding follows a predominantly Langmuir-type, saturation-controlled process, with secondary Ru accumulation appearing at high loading. The work establishes lactoferrin as a promising protein-based coordination scaffold for Ru(III) delivery and biomedical applications.
