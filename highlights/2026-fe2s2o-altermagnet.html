Valley Polarization and Symmetry-Driven Spin Splitting in Altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO Monolayers and Bilayers

Materials Today Physics 67, 102198 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

A. Gauswami, B. R. Dhori, P. K. Jha, "Valley polarization and symmetry-driven spin splitting in altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers," Materials Today Physics 67, 102198 (2026).

DOI: 10.1016/j.mtphys.2026.102198

We present a first-principles study of altermagnetism in Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers, revealing tunable valley polarization and piezomagnetism. The intrinsically symmetry-broken Fe2SSeO bilayer exhibits robust momentum-dependent spin splitting, highlighting its potential for 2D spintronic and valleytronic applications.
