Electric Field-Induced Bond Length and Bandgap Tunability for Enhanced Reactivity of Nitride Nanorings

Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 28(24), 14540–14550 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, "Electric field-induced bond length and bandgap tunability for enhanced reactivity of nitride nanorings," Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 28(24), 14540–14550 (2026).

DOI: 10.1039/d6cp01438k

This study presents a comprehensive DFT investigation of Al9N9 and B9N9 nanorings under an external electric field to control their structural and electronic properties. Increasing electric field induces bond elongation, ring contraction, charge redistribution, and increasing dipole moment, while Al9N9 retains its cyclic structure across the studied field range. Al9N9 exhibits a stable field-tunable response, whereas B9N9 undergoes significant structural distortion at higher fields. The study highlights Al9N9 as a robust electric-field-tunable semiconductor with potential applications in nanoelectronics, sensing, and field-controlled molecular devices.
