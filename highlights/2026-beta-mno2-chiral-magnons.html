Strain-Controlled Chiral Magnons and Spin-Dependent Seebeck Effect in Tetragonal β-MnO2 Altermagnet

A DFT study · Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 025802 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "Strain-controlled chiral magnons and spin-dependent Seebeck effect in tetragonal β-MnO2 altermagnet: a DFT study," Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 025802 (2026).

DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/ae3353

We present a first-principles study of chiral magnons and spin-dependent transport in tetragonal β-MnO2, revealing nonreciprocal magnon dispersion and tunable spin splitting under strain. These results establish β-MnO2 as a promising material for field-free spintronic and magnonic applications.
