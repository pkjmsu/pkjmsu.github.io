A Unified Review of Altermagnetism: Momentum-Space Spin Polarization and Emerging Applications

Small Methods, e70991 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "A Unified Review of Altermagnetism: Momentum-Space Spin Polarization and Emerging Applications," Small Methods, e70991 (2026).

DOI: 10.1002/smtd.70991

Altermagnetism is a distinct class of collinear magnetism characterized by zero net magnetization but symmetry-driven, momentum-dependent spin splitting. This review covers its fundamental principles, symmetry, materials, experimental identification, computational approaches, and emerging applications in spintronics, valleytronics, magnonics, orbitronics, superconductivity, and quantum technologies.
