Research highlight · 2026

Revisiting the High-Pressure Phase Transitions of Yttrium

Insights from density functional theory · Physical Review B 113, 184108 (2026)

← Back to Highlights

P. Patel, M. H. Dalsaniya, S. Patel, D. Kurzydłowski, K. J. Kurzydłowski, P. K. Jha, "Revisiting the high-pressure phase transitions of yttrium: Insights from density functional theory," Physical Review B 113, 184108 (2026).

DOI: 10.1103/jp7h-d7q3

This work provides a first-principles understanding of the pressure-induced structural phase transitions in yttrium below 30 GPa. Using the r2SCAN functional, the experimentally observed transition pressures are accurately reproduced, while phonon and elastic analyses reveal vibrational and mechanical softening as key drivers of the transitions. A previously unidentified P̄62c intermediate phase is predicted between the hcp and Sm-type phases, highlighting the role of soft-mode dynamics in high-pressure structural transformations. The study further connects these transitions with pressure-induced s-to-d electronic charge transfer and evolving chemical bonding.
