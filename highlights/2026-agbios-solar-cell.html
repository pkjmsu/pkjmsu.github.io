Investigation on Electronic, Optical, Thermal and Photovoltaic Properties of AgBiOS Oxychalcogenide

N. Joshi, N. A. Tukadiya, A. Pandya, P. K. Jha, "Investigation on electronic, optical, thermal and photovoltaic properties of AgBiOS oxychalcogenide," Solar Energy 312, 114580 (2026).

DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2026.114580

This study combines DFT and SCAPS-1D to investigate AgBiOS as a lead-free solar-cell absorber. AgBiOS shows a 1.26 eV band gap, strong visible-light absorption, and a simulated power conversion efficiency of 21.98% in an FTO/TiO2/AgBiOS/CuI device. The results also reveal good defect tolerance and provide guidelines for efficient thin-film photovoltaic design.
