Machine Learning and SCAPS-1D Based Prediction and Validation of RbGeBr3 Perovskite Solar Cell

Solar Energy

← Back to Highlights

"Machine learning and SCAPS-1D based prediction and validation of RbGeBr3 perovskite solar cell," Solar Energy.

DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2025.113760

This study combines machine learning and SCAPS-1D to predict the photovoltaic performance of RbGeBr3 perovskite. XGBoost predicted a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 30.67%, while SCAPS-1D validation achieved 31.76%. The results demonstrate the potential of ML-assisted screening for designing high-efficiency perovskite solar cells.
