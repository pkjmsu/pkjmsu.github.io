Research

Computational materials science, first-principles DFT, and machine-learning-assisted design

Topological Quantum Materials

Exploration of topological insulating phases, Dirac and Weyl semimetals, Rashba systems, and non-trivial topology in non-centrosymmetric compounds, half-Heusler alloys, and two-dimensional materials — including topological transitions driven by pressure, strain and volume expansion. The group also studies ferroelectric Rashba topological phases (e.g. CsSiBi) and composite quantum materials hosting giant Rashba effects, topological phases and electric-polarization control.

Two-Dimensional Materials

First-principles investigation of graphene, graphynes, hexagonal boron nitride, phosphorene, transition-metal dichalcogenides (MoS2, WS2, SnSe2, …), Janus monolayers, MXenes, and other low-dimensional systems — their electronic, vibrational, mechanical and optical properties, along with defect and doping engineering for catalytic (HER/OER) and sensing applications.

Energy Materials & Hydrogen Storage

Design of thermoelectric materials, solar-cell absorbers (perovskites, chalcopyrites, oxychalcogenides), and hydrogen-storage and single-atom-catalyst systems. Topics include machine-learned force fields for energy-materials discovery, high-energy-density materials, and CO2 reduction and fuel-cell-related materials.

Nanophononics & Phonon Engineering

Vibrational and mechanical properties of nanoparticles, biological objects and hybrid nanostructures; phonon quantization and low-frequency Raman spectroscopy of free and embedded semiconductor nanoparticles, nano-structured CMR materials, and viruses. Includes high-pressure phonon dynamics and structural phase transitions from model theory and first-principles techniques.

Machine-Learning Materials Design

Combining density functional theory with machine learning for high-throughput discovery of thermoelectric materials, prediction of lattice thermal conductivity, adsorption energies over bimetallic surfaces, and band gaps of perovskites — accelerating materials discovery.

High-Pressure Behaviour of Solids

Calculations of phonon dynamics, structural and pressure-driven phase transitions, elastic and thermodynamic properties of semiconductors, complex oxides, nuclear materials, and geologically relevant (hydrous) minerals using both model theory and ab-initio methods.

Projects

The group has undertaken 38 projects — 26 as Principal Investigator, 4 as Co-PI and 8 as mentor — funded by DST, SERB, UGC, DAE-BRNS, DST-FIST, ANRF, MOES, and international agencies (ICM/Poland, DST Indo-Poland, DST Indo-Sri Lanka, DST-RFBR, DST Indo-Croatia). Notable ongoing projects include the ANRF-PAIR project (with Hub IIT Bombay) and the DST-FIST Physical Science project.
