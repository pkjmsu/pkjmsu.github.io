2026 Publication Highlights

Key findings from the group's 2026 publications, sorted by latest month

2026 Highlights

  • November 2026 — Journal of Power Sources 692, 240949
    S. Mishra, R. Sainda, S. Mishra, D. Vinod, P. K. Jha, V. Kulshrestha, "Rewiring hydration topology enables polarization enhanced proton transport in PFSA membranes." DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.240949 · Read more →

    Highlight: A melamine–H3BTC hydrogen-bonded organic framework (1 wt%) reworks the hydration network of PFSA membranes, boosting proton conductivity to 9.4×10⁻² S cm⁻¹ at 30 °C and 1.0×10⁻¹ S cm⁻¹ at 80 °C.

  • September 2026 — Materials Today Physics 67, 102198
    A. Gauswami, B. R. Dhori, P. K. Jha, "Valley polarization and symmetry-driven spin splitting in altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers." DOI: 10.1016/j.mtphys.2026.102198 · Read more →

    Highlight: Robust valley polarization and symmetry-driven spin splitting persist in altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers, with piezomagnetic coupling for magnetoelastic control.

  • August 2026 — Small Methods, e70991
    A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "A Unified Review of Altermagnetism: Momentum-Space Spin Polarization and Emerging Applications." DOI: 10.1002/smtd.70991 · Read more →

    Highlight: A unified review of altermagnetism — a magnetic class combining zero net magnetization with momentum-dependent spin splitting — covering theory, materials and emerging device applications.

  • August 2026 — ACS Applied Energy Materials 9(17), 11454–11465
    N. A. Tukadiya, P. K. Jha, "Investigation on the Electronic, Optical, Mechanical, and Photovoltaic Properties of CuZn2InS4 Quaternary Chalcogenide." DOI: 10.1021/acsaem.6c01592

    Highlight: HSE06 calculations give a direct band gap of 1.94 eV; an Al/FTO/CdS/CuZn2InS4/Cu2O/Ni solar-cell stack reaches 18.72% power conversion efficiency.

  • July 2026 — Solar Energy 312, 114580
    N. Joshi, N. A. Tukadiya, A. Pandya, P. K. Jha, "Investigation on electronic, optical, thermal and photovoltaic properties of AgBiOS oxychalcogenide." DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2026.114580 · Read more →

    Highlight: AgBiOS shows a direct band gap of 1.26 eV; an FTO/TiO2/AgBiOS/CuI device achieves a power conversion efficiency of 21.98%.

  • June 2026 — Physical Chemistry Chemical Physics 28(24), 14540–14550
    R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, "Electric field-induced bond length and bandgap tunability for enhanced reactivity of nitride nanorings." DOI: 10.1039/d6cp01438k · Read more →

    Highlight: Field-tunable semiconductor behaviour in Al9N9/B9N9 nitride nanorings, where an applied electric field elongates bonds and contracts the ring to boost chemical reactivity.

  • June 2026 — 2D Materials 13(3), 032002
    H. T. Nair, Z. D. Ponkiya, D. Chodvadiya, P. K. Jha, B. Chakraborty, "Emerging frontiers of MXenes: structure, properties and energy applications." DOI: 10.1088/2053-1583/ae726b · Read more →

    Highlight: A review of MXenes covering their structure, MAX-phase etching synthesis, and applications in the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) and energy storage.

  • May 2026 — Physical Review B 113, 184108
    P. Patel, M. H. Dalsaniya, S. Patel, D. Kurzydłowski, K. J. Kurzydłowski, P. K. Jha, "Revisiting the high-pressure phase transitions of yttrium: Insights from density functional theory." DOI: 10.1103/jp7h-d7q3 · Read more →

    Highlight: r2SCAN reproduces the experimentally known transition pressures of yttrium (<30 GPa), predicts a new P-62c intermediate phase between the hcp and Sm-type structures, and reveals the s-to-d charge transfer driving the phase transitions.

  • April 2026 — Inorganic Chemistry Frontiers 13(11), 4867–4897
    T. Dyrda-Terniuk, M. Ehlert, K. Roszak, G. Trykowski, K. Szpotkowski, Ł. Skowroński, O. Pryshchepa, R. van Eldik, K. Mizgalska, W. Guida, A. Karolak, R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, P. Pomastowski, "Lactoferrin as an active coordination scaffold for ruthenium (III)." DOI: 10.1039/d6qi00255b · Read more →

    Highlight: Lactoferrin acts as a Langmuir-type active coordination scaffold for ruthenium (III).

  • January 2026 — Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 035501
    S. Patel, P. K. Jha, "Ferroelectric Rashba Topological Phase in Noncentrosymmetric CsSiBi." DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/AE3511 · Read more →

    Highlight: A topological insulating phase coexisting with isotropic Rashba spin splitting in CsSiBi, with ferroelectric switching offering electric-field control of the spin topology.

  • January 2026 — Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 38, 025802
    A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "Strain-controlled chiral magnons and spin-dependent Seebeck effect in tetragonal β-MnO2 altermagnet: a DFT study." DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/ae3353 · Read more →

    Highlight: Strain-controlled chiral magnons with nonreciprocal dispersion and tunable spin splitting in a tetragonal β-MnO2 altermagnet, together with an orientational spin-dependent Seebeck effect.

  • 2026 — Solar Energy
    "Machine learning and SCAPS-1D based prediction and validation of RbGeBr3 perovskite solar cell." DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2025.113760 · Read more →

    Highlight: XGBoost predicts a power conversion efficiency of 30.67% for RbGeBr3 perovskite, validated by SCAPS-1D at 31.76%, demonstrating the potential of ML-assisted screening for high-efficiency perovskite solar cells.
