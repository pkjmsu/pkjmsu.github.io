Recent Publication Highlights

Key findings from the group's 2025–2026 publications, arranged by published date

2025–2026 Highlights

  1. November 2025 — Solar Energy 300, 113760
    N. A. Tukadiya, Z. D. Ponkiya, N. Joshi, D. Upadhyay, P. K. Jha, "Machine learning and SCAPS-1D based prediction and validation of RbGeBr3 perovskite solar cell." DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2025.113760

    Highlight: Machine-learning (XGBoost) prediction of power conversion efficiency reaching 30.67%, validated by SCAPS-1D device simulation to 31.76%.

  2. 12 January 2026 — J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 38, 025802
    A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "Strain-controlled chiral magnons and spin-dependent Seebeck effect in tetragonal β-MnO2 altermagnet: a DFT study." DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/ae3353

    Highlight: Chiral magnons with nonreciprocal dispersion in β-MnO2, robust against crystal symmetry; strain acts as a switch — compressive strain enhances while tensile strain suppresses spin-splitting in the magnon spectra, with orientational spin-dependent Seebeck transport.

  3. 21 January 2026 — J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 38, 035501
    S. Patel, P. K. Jha, "Ferroelectric Rashba Topological Phase in Noncentrosymmetric CsSiBi." DOI: 10.1088/1361-648X/AE3511

    Highlight: A topological insulating phase coexisting with isotropic Rashba spin splitting in CsSiBi, with ferroelectric switching offering electric-field control of the spin topology.

  4. 24 April 2026 (online) — Inorganic Chemistry Frontiers 13(11), 4867–4897
    T. Dyrda-Terniuk, M. Ehlert, K. Roszak, G. Trykowski, K. Szpotkowski, Ł. Skowroński, O. Pryshchepa, R. van Eldik, K. Mizgalska, W. Guida, A. Karolak, R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, P. Pomastowski, "Lactoferrin as an active coordination scaffold for ruthenium (III)." DOI: 10.1039/d6qi00255b

    Highlight: Lactoferrin serves as a Langmuir-type active coordination scaffold for Ru(III), anchoring the metal ion through its side-chain residues.

  5. 6 May 2026 — Physical Review B 113, 184108
    P. Patel, M. H. Dalsaniya, S. Patel, D. Kurzydłowski, K. J. Kurzydłowski, P. K. Jha, "Revisiting the high-pressure phase transitions of yttrium: Insights from density functional theory." DOI: 10.1103/jp7h-d7q3

    Highlight: r2SCAN reproduces the experimentally known transition pressures of yttrium (<30 GPa), predicts a new P-62c intermediate phase between the hcp and Sm-type structures, and reveals the s-to-d charge transfer that drives the pressure-driven phase transitions.

  6. 19 June 2026 (online) — 2D Materials 13(3), 032002
    H. T. Nair, Z. D. Ponkiya, D. Chodvadiya, P. K. Jha, B. Chakraborty, "Emerging frontiers of MXenes: structure, properties and energy applications." DOI: 10.1088/2053-1583/ae726b

    Highlight: A review covering the structural diversity, MAX-phase etching synthesis, and the hydrogen-evolution and energy-storage applications of MXenes.

  7. June 2026 (issue 24) — Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 28(24), 14540–14550
    R. Sainda, P. K. Jha, "Electric field-induced bond length and bandgap tunability for enhanced reactivity of nitride nanorings." DOI: 10.1039/d6cp01438k

    Highlight: An applied electric field continuously tunes the bandgap of Al9N9/B9N9 nitride nanorings from insulating to semiconducting, elongating bonds and contracting the ring to boost chemical reactivity.

  8. 1 July 2026 — Solar Energy 312, 114580
    N. Joshi, N. A. Tukadiya, A. Pandya, P. K. Jha, "Investigation on electronic, optical, thermal and photovoltaic properties of AgBiOS oxychalcogenide." DOI: 10.1016/j.solener.2026.114580

    Highlight: AgBiOS shows a direct band gap of 1.26 eV; an FTO/TiO2/AgBiOS/CuI device achieves a power conversion efficiency of 21.98%.

  9. 17 August 2026 (online) — ACS Applied Energy Materials 9(17), 11454–11465
    N. A. Tukadiya, P. K. Jha, "Investigation on the Electronic, Optical, Mechanical, and Photovoltaic Properties of CuZn2InS4 Quaternary Chalcogenide." DOI: 10.1021/acsaem.6c01592

    Highlight: HSE06 calculations give a direct band gap of 1.94 eV; an Al/FTO/CdS/CuZn2InS4/Cu2O/Ni solar-cell stack reaches 18.72% power conversion efficiency.

  10. 31 August 2026 (online) — Small Methods, e70991
    A. Gauswami, P. K. Jha, "A Unified Review of Altermagnetism: Momentum-Space Spin Polarization and Emerging Applications." DOI: 10.1002/smtd.70991

    Highlight: A unified review of altermagnetism — a magnetic class combining zero net magnetization with momentum-dependent spin splitting — covering theory, materials and emerging device applications.

  11. September 2026 — Materials Today Physics 67, 102198
    A. Gauswami, B. R. Dhori, P. K. Jha, "Valley polarization and symmetry-driven spin splitting in altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers." DOI: 10.1016/j.mtphys.2026.102198

    Highlight: Robust valley polarization and symmetry-driven spin splitting persist in altermagnetic Fe2S2O and Janus Fe2SSeO monolayers and bilayers, with piezomagnetic coupling for magnetoelastic control.

  12. 15 November 2026 — Journal of Power Sources 692, 240949
    S. Mishra, R. Sainda, S. Mishra, D. Vinod, P. K. Jha, V. Kulshrestha, "Rewiring hydration topology enables polarization enhanced proton transport in PFSA membranes." DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.240949

    Highlight: A melamine–H3BTC hydrogen-bonded organic framework (1 wt%) reworks the hydration network of PFSA membranes, boosting proton conductivity to 9.4×10⁻² S cm⁻¹ at 30 °C and 1.0×10⁻¹ S cm⁻¹ at 80 °C.
