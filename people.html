Principal Investigator

PKJ

Prof. Prafulla K. Jha

Senior Professor, Department of Physics · Head of Department (since Nov 2021) · Director of Research (since Oct 2025)

Ph.D., Condensed Matter Physics, Barkatullah University, Bhopal (1992). Research interests span computational (first-principles DFT) materials science — graphene and carbon allotropes, boron nitride, nanoparticles and nanowires, complex oxides, nuclear materials, transition-metal alloys and heterostructures — together with low-frequency Raman spectroscopy, high-pressure physics, phonon engineering and machine-learning-assisted materials design.
